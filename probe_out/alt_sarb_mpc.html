Monetary Policy Committee

The SARB’s Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) conducts monetary policy within a flexible inflation-targeting framework

 

The MPC consists of up to seven members from the SARB: the Governor, the three deputy governors and selected senior officials appointed by the Governor. The Governor chairs the MPC meetings.
